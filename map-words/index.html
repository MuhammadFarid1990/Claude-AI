How the real system works
  • Runs on one laptop (16 GB RAM, Mac with M chip or Windows with NVIDIA graphics). No internet needed at the event.
  • A small reading model (GLM-OCR) reads handwriting, then a language model (Qwen3-VL) fixes spelling and grammar while keeping the student's words.
  • No child's words or photos leave the laptop. Photos are deleted once the helper approves or rejects them.
  • Nothing appears on the projector until an adult approves it.
  • All software and models are free. The projector page is plain HTML, so its look can be designed for each event.
