Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Short version: Bloom stores all your data on your own device. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal health information.

1. What data Bloom collects

Bloom is designed with privacy first. All data you enter — your name, due date, pregnancy details, meal logs, symptoms, mood entries, kick counts, and goals — is stored locally on your device using localStorage. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to share it.

2. Data we do not collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store:

  • Your name, pregnancy details, or health data
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Analytics or usage telemetry
  • Cookies or tracking pixels

3. Children's privacy

Bloom is designed for expectant and new parents. It is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

4. Health information disclaimer

Bloom provides general information for informational purposes only. Daily meals, exercises, baby activities, first-food suggestions and symptom guidance are based on publicly available guidelines from organisations including the American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists (ACOG), the American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP), the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) and the World Health Organization (WHO). This content has not been individually reviewed by a doctor, dietitian or other clinician, and it is not personalised medical advice.

Bloom is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult your doctor, midwife or other qualified healthcare provider before starting a new exercise programme, changing your diet, or making decisions about your or your baby's health, and follow their advice where it differs from Bloom's. Stop any activity and seek care if something feels wrong. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

You use the information in Bloom at your own discretion and risk.

5. Third-party services

Bloom uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Hosting (Google) — serves the app's files. Google may log standard request metadata (IP address, timestamp) as part of hosting.
  • Google Fonts — loads typeface files for the app interface.

6. Data retention and deletion

Since all app data is stored on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data or uninstalling the app. We have no server-side copy to delete.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy at this URL with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of Bloom after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
