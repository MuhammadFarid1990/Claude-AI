**Description** (4000 chars max)
```
Bloom is your all-in-one pregnancy and parenting companion — from the first trimester to toddlerhood. Beautiful, warm, and built around you.

PREGNANCY TRACKING
Know exactly where you are in your journey. Week-by-week baby size comparisons, milestone cards, and a progress bar that shows how far you've come. Bloom updates automatically so you always see what's happening right now — at week 18 your baby is a sweet potato; at week 30, a cabbage.

MEALS & NUTRITION
Personalized calorie targets based on your body and trimester. Log breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks with a tap. Track macros (protein, carbs, fat) and see your weekly pattern across 7 days. Bloom adapts to your dietary preferences — Mediterranean, vegetarian, dairy-free, and more.

HEALTH LOG
• Symptom Checker — tap what you're feeling, get instant guidance on whether it's normal and when to call your doctor
• Kick Counter — tap to count fetal movements with a weekly log
• Mood Tracker — check in daily on how you're feeling emotionally and physically

SCHEDULE & MILESTONES
A trimester-by-trimester timeline of what to expect: appointments, tests, vaccines, and recovery milestones. Switch between First, Second, and Third Trimester views. Never miss a glucose test or anatomy scan.

ME — YOUR PROFILE
Track your weight, BMI, age, and dietary goals. Edit your profile anytime. Keep a running list of questions for your next doctor's appointment — so you never walk in and forget what you wanted to ask.

BLOOM AI CHAT 🌸
Ask anything — nutrition questions, what a symptom means, how to sleep better in the third trimester, what to pack in your hospital bag. Bloom AI is a warm, knowledgeable assistant trained on pregnancy and parenting. It gives real answers, recommends seeing a doctor for serious concerns, and never makes you feel judged for asking.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
All your data stays on your device. Bloom never sends your health information to any server. The only data that leaves your phone is your chat messages, processed securely to generate AI responses and immediately discarded.

Bloom is designed for expectant parents who want clarity, not chaos — a calm, beautiful companion for the most important journey of your life.
```

## App Review Notes (for Apple reviewer)

```
This app is a pregnancy tracking and parenting information app.

To test the app without creating an account:
1. Tap "⚡ Try Demo" on the welcome screen — this launches the full app immediately with sample data.

The AI chat feature requires a network connection and calls our Vercel API endpoint (which uses the Groq language model). It will work on a device with internet access.

All user health data is stored locally on the device (localStorage). No account or login is required to use the app.

There is no in-app purchase, subscription, or monetization in this version.
```
